## [Bulls and Cows: The Summoning](https://yaroslavagd.github.io/bulls-and-cows/)

Summon a demon by guessing its secret sigil. Each try tells you how many symbols are right and in place, and how many are right but misplaced. No attempt limit, but speed counts.

Bulls and Cows: The Summoning — the old number-guessing game turned into a dark ritual. You're a demon summoner and the demon's true name is a sequence of unique pictograms. Pick the length and the numeral system, enter your guess and read the hint: bulls are right symbols in the right place, cows are right symbols in the wrong one. No attempt limit, but fewer tries and less time put you higher in the records table.

## [Minesweeper](https://yaroslavagd.github.io/rs-school-minesweeper/)

The classic mine-clearing puzzle. Open cells, read the numbers, flag the suspicious ones and try not to blow up. One wrong click and it's over.

Minesweeper — the classic. Mines are hidden across a grid of cells that can be closed, opened or flagged. Open a cell and it shows how many mines are around it, or a blank and a whole area opens up. Flag the cells you think are mined, and don't open a real one, because that's a loss.

## [RSS CSS Selectors](https://yaroslavagd.github.io/rs-school-rss-css-selectors/)

A little trainer for CSS selectors. Some elements on the table pulse, you write a selector that hits exactly those. Stuck? There's a Help button that types the answer for you.

RSS CSS Selectors — a trainer for learning CSS selectors, inspired by CSS Diner. Every level shows a bit of markup with some elements pulsing, and you write a selector that matches exactly those. Hover the code or the picture to see what matches what, hit Help if you're stuck, and your progress is saved between visits.

## [Memory Game: Neither This Nor That](https://yaroslavagd.github.io/memory-game/)

A vegetable dark fantasy memory game. Eight princes court a princess, she rejects every pair you find and eats them. The last one left wins her hand.

Memory Game: Neither This Nor That — a memory game in vegetable dark fantasy. Eight vegetable princes are after the princess's hand. Every pair you find gets rejected and eaten, and the last prince standing becomes her chosen one. Each eaten prince has his own story and verdict. Every flip counts as a move, so fewer moves puts you higher in the Court Chronicle.

## [Moosweep](https://yaroslavagd.github.io/moosweep/)

You're a gourmet cow on a 9x9 field. Eat all the grass you can, but watch out for the mines. Your sensitive nose tells you how many are nearby.

Moosweep — a minesweeper-like game about a gourmet cow looking for freedom. The field is 9x9, split into nine 3x3 squares with one mine hidden in each, in new places every run. Walk in four directions, eat the grass and trust your nose: it shows how many mines are around you. Step on one and it's over.

## [Pair 'em Up](https://yaroslavagd.github.io/pair-em-up/)

A number-matching puzzle on a 9-column grid. Remove pairs that are equal or add up to 10 and reach 100 points before you run out of moves and helpers.

Pair 'em Up — a number-matching puzzle. Clear the grid by removing pairs of identical numbers or numbers that add up to 10, and reach 100 points before you run out of moves. Three modes: Classic, Random and Chaotic. Helpers like Hints, Revert, Add Numbers, Shuffle and Eraser are limited, so use them wisely.

## [Virtual Music Kit](https://yaroslavagd.github.io/virtual-music-kit/)

Play a virtual instrument with your mouse or keyboard. Seven sounds, keys you can rebind, and a sequence field to play whole melodies automatically.

Virtual Music Kit — a small interactive instrument you play in the browser. Click the keys or press the assigned letters on your keyboard to make sounds, and rebind any key you don't like. You can also type a sequence of keys and hit play to listen to your melody one note at a time.

## Forest Guardians (coming soon)

A small tactical game about saving a forest from spreading fire. Start with one scout squirrel, find the towns, hire helpers and put the flames out. Coming soon.

Forest Guardians — a small tactical game about saving the forest from a spreading fire. You start with a single squirrel scout on a 10x10 top-down map, find the towns, hire a hedgehog or a turtle and use their water to put the fire out. Hold on for 25 turns and keep at least 3 of 5 towns. If a town burns before you reach it, you can never hire there again.
